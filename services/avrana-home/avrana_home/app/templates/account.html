Signing in

Avrana and each library you open from it have their own sign‑in. Some libraries offer an Avrana sign‑in button; others ask for a username and password. If a library asks you to sign in, that is expected. Help has the steps for each library and device.

Signing out

Sign out, below, signs you out of Avrana Media on this device and clears what it saved here. Apps and libraries you signed in to separately stay signed in until you sign out inside each one. On a shared device, sign out of each.

Getting help

Open Help for your devices and libraries. If something is still wrong, ask the person who invited you.
