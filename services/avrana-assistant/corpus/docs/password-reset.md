# Forgot your password

Passwords are reset only through the Avrana password reset flow, titled "Reset your Avrana password".
This assistant cannot reset a password, cannot see a password, and will never ask you for one. Do not
type your password into this chat.

## How the reset works

1. Identify yourself with your username or your email address.
2. Avrana emails you a link. The link expires after 30 minutes.
3. Open the link and enter a new password twice.

## Things to know

- A reset changes your main Avrana password. A few services keep a separate copy of your password that
  a reset does not update. If a service stops accepting your new password, check that service's help
  page or contact support.
- Whether email reset is switched on for your account today is not something this assistant can see.
  The welcome guide says self-service reset by email was not set up yet at the time it was written. If
  the email does not arrive, contact support.
