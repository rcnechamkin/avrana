# Requesting an ebook or audiobook

Open the Request tile on your Avrana home page and choose the option for an ebook or audiobook. The
direct address is https://requestabook.avrana.net.

The request option for books is available to members who have ebook or audiobook access. Once added,
the title appears in the ebook or audiobook library.

Step-by-step instructions for the request form are not available to this assistant yet. If you get
stuck, contact support and say which title you were trying to request.
