# Ebooks: how to read, download and send to a Kindle

Address: https://books.avrana.net

On the login page, type your Avrana username and password directly. This service has no single
sign-on button. After signing in, open a book to read it in the browser, or use the download button.

## Send to Kindle

1. Set your Kindle address once at https://kindle.avrana.net.
2. On Amazon, add the Avrana sender address to your approved sender list. The Books help page on your
   Avrana home page shows the address to approve.
3. In the library, open a book and choose Send to Kindle.

## Which app to install

- iPhone and iPad: the Kindle app from the App Store. Books sent from the library appear in the Kindle
  app automatically. No server address is needed.
- Android: the Kindle app from Google Play works the same way. Alternatively use ReadEra from Google
  Play: download a book from https://books.avrana.net and open the downloaded file in ReadEra.
- Any browser: https://books.avrana.net.
