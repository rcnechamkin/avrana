# Movies and TV: how to watch and which app to install

Address: https://watch.avrana.net

Sign in with your Avrana username and password. There is no single sign-on button for Movies and TV:
type the username and password you created with your account.

## Which app to install

Use an app where possible. Playing in a web browser is the least reliable option.

- iPhone and iPad: Swiftfin (recommended) or Jellyfin Mobile, both from the App Store.
- Android phone or tablet: Jellyfin from Google Play.
- Roku: search the Roku Channel Store for Jellyfin, add the channel and open it.
- Fire TV and Fire Stick: search for Jellyfin on the Fire TV home screen and install it.
- Google TV, Android TV and NVIDIA Shield: search for Jellyfin in the Play Store on the television.
- Apple TV: search for Swiftfin in the App Store on the Apple TV.
- Samsung and LG smart TVs: search the television's own app store for Jellyfin. If it is not
  available, use a Roku, Fire Stick or Apple TV instead, or cast from a phone.
- Windows: Jellyfin Media Player from jellyfin.org. It handles more video formats than a browser, so
  it is the better choice on a computer.
- Any browser: open https://watch.avrana.net and sign in.

In every app, the steps are the same: install it, add the server https://watch.avrana.net, and sign
in with your Avrana username and password.

## Other options mentioned in the welcome guide

The welcome guide also recommends Wholphin, a Jellyfin app with a Netflix-style layout, on Android TV,
Google TV and Fire TV. It describes Quick Connect for televisions: choose Quick Connect in the app to
get a 6-character code, then on your phone open https://watch.avrana.net, go to your profile, choose
Quick Connect and enter the code. That means no typing a password with a remote. The welcome guide
was written before the September recovery and has not been re-checked line by line.

## One thing that is not automatic

Your Movies and TV password is a separate copy made when you signed up. If you later change your main
Avrana password, Movies and TV keeps the old one until that is sorted out. If you get stuck here,
contact support.
