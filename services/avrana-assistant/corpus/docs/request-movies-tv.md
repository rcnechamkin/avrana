# Requesting a film or show

Address: https://request.avrana.net

1. Open the address and choose Sign in with Jellyfin. Your account is created automatically the first
   time. You sign in with your Avrana username and password.
2. Search for a title and choose Request.
3. It appears in Movies and TV once it has been added.

## Apps

- iPhone and iPad: Pocket for Seerr from the App Store. Add the server https://request.avrana.net and
  sign in with your Avrana username and password.
- Android: there is no recommended app yet. Open https://request.avrana.net in a browser and add it to
  the home screen for one-tap access.
- Any browser: https://request.avrana.net.
