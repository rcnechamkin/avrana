# Getting started with your Avrana account

Avrana is a small, private media service shared with friends and family. You get one Avrana account
that works across the services you have been given.

- You create the account from an invite link: pick a username and a password and enter your email.
- Your Avrana home page is at https://avrana.net. It shows a tile for each service your account has
  access to, and only those. Click a tile to open that service. Bookmark the home page.
- How you sign in depends on the service. Some show a "Sign in with Avrana" button. Others ask you to
  type your Avrana username and password directly. Each service's help page says which.
- The home page has a Help area with step-by-step setup for the services on your account.
- Keep your username and password somewhere safe. A password manager is ideal. Avrana support will
  never ask you to send your password in a message, and neither will this assistant.

If you are not sure what you should have access to, see "Why can't I see a library?" or contact support.
