# Requesting a comic or manga

Open the Request tile on your Avrana home page and choose the option for a comic or manga. The direct
address is https://requestacomic.avrana.net.

The comic request option is available to members who have comics access. Once added, the title appears
in the comics library.

Step-by-step instructions for the request form are not available to this assistant yet. If you get
stuck, contact support and say which title you were trying to request.
