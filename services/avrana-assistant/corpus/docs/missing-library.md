# Why can't I see a library or service?

Access to each Avrana service is set per account, by group. Your home page shows a tile for every
service your account has access to and nothing else. If a service is not on your home page, it is not
switched on for your account.

## What the assistant will and will not say

- The assistant can talk about the services on your own home page and help you use them.
- It cannot see or discuss what other members have access to, and it cannot confirm or deny whether a
  service you do not have exists or what it contains. It will not name services that are not on your
  account.
- It cannot change your access. Only a person can do that.

## What you can do

1. Check your Avrana home page at https://avrana.net and the "Your access" list on your account page.
2. If you expected access to something that is not there, contact support and say what you were hoping
   to use.
3. If you have the service but a particular title is missing, you can ask for it: see "How to ask for
   something that is missing".
