### U-01 Reduce Motion draws the scrolled strip on top of the resting hero

- **Class / severity:** CONFIRMED, P2. **Beta-blocking: yes.** Every member with iOS Reduce Motion on sees Home's
  first screen garbled (title twice, overlapping progress text, two play buttons); the fix is a few CSS lines.
- **User impact:** the most prominent thing on Home is broken for a common accessibility setting. The Resume button
  is half covered by the strip's round play link (same destination, so tapping still works).
- **Reproduction:** `/`, member-b, 390x844, `ok`, `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`; load, do nothing.
- **Expected:** the at-rest hero (Round 10 9A) exactly as without the preference, with no motion.
- **Actual:** `.hero-mini` and `.hero-play` are `display:flex; opacity:1; visibility:visible` at scroll 0: the title
  "Synthetic Film Two" appears twice, "81 minutes left" is drawn twice, offset, and the round play button overlaps
  "Resume". The window also stays full height and scrolls away (no strip), which is acceptable.
- **Baseline 2cb228f.** **Evidence:** `home-hero-rest-390-rm.png` vs `home-hero-rest-390.png`; `interact.json`
  `hero-rm[0]`: `mini flex/1/visible`, `play flex/1`.
- **Root cause:** `home.css:466` turns the strip pieces on (`.hero-mini,.hero-play{display:flex;animation:hero-in
  ...}`) and relies on the animation's fill to hide them; `home.css:100` (`*{animation:none!important}` under
  reduce) removes the animation, so nothing hides them.
- **Minimum correction:** after the `@supports` block, `@media (prefers-reduced-motion:reduce){.hero-mini,
  .hero-play{display:none}.hero-stick{position:relative;top:auto}}`. (Alternative, owner's call: exempt the hero's
  scroll-linked timeline from the global kill, since it moves only with the finger.)
- **Regression risk:** low; scoped to the reduce query. Add a webtest that asserts the rule exists, and a browser
  check in `check_home_export.mjs` with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`.
- **Existing issue:** none found (PR #159 verified scroll frames only without the preference).

### U-02 Increase Contrast and forced colours push the hero over the "Continue" heading

- **Class / severity:** CONFIRMED, P2. **Beta-blocking: yes.** iOS "Increase Contrast" maps to `prefers-contrast:
  more` in Safari; with it the Continue heading is hidden under the hero card, a one-line fix.
- **Reproduction:** `/`, member-b, 390x844, `contrast: 'more'` or `forcedColors: 'active'`.
- **Expected:** the flat raised title card (PR #159: "more contrast / forced colours drop the picture"), then
  Continue.
- **Actual:** `.hero` is 135 px tall at y 139..274, but `.hero-stick` is drawn at 197..332, 58 px lower, over
  `#h-continue` (302..327); `elementFromPoint` on the heading returns `.hero-bar`. Extra empty space above the card.
- **Evidence:** `home-hc-390.png`, `home-fc-390.png` (the heading is missing between the hero and the first
  cards); `fc.mjs` output.
- **Root cause:** `home.css:460` gives `.hero-stick` `top:var(--head-t)` (and line 469 `top:var(--head-h)` when
  scrolled) for `position:sticky`; the overrides at `home.css:475` and `:479` change it to `position:relative` but keep
  `top`, so it becomes a 58 px relative offset.
- **Minimum correction:** add `top:auto` to `.hero-stick` in both override blocks, with enough specificity to beat
  `html[data-scrolled]:not([data-chrome="tucked"]) .hero-stick` (for example repeat that selector, or `top:auto
  !important` there).
- **Regression risk:** low. **Existing issue:** none.

### U-03 RC1 A11Y-1 reproduced: at 200 % text a focused rail card is not wholly in view

- **Class / severity:** CONFIRMED, P2. **Beta-blocking: no.** Keyboard and switch users at large text see the
  focused card cut in half; the ring and the title start are visible and Enter works, so there is a workaround.
- **Reproduction:** `/`, member-b (also member-a, `?for=watch|listen|read`, `/library`), 390x844,
  `html{font-size:200%}`, Tab from the top.
- **Measured:** the second card of every rail is focused at **308..548 px inside a rail box of 0..390 px**
  (`clientWidth` 390): **162 px of a 240 px card clipped** (234 px of a 312 px still on member-a's Continue);
  `scrollLeft` unchanged. 16 to 6 failing stops per page; also at **430@200 %** (16 on Home). **Not** reproduced at
  360 or 390 at 100 %, at 390@150 %, at 320@200 % (cards cap at 80vw) or at 195x422 (Safari-style reflow). The
  repository's own `check_home_export.mjs` fails the same way on 16 exported Home pages (`check_home_export.log`:
  "Tab 308..548 in 0..390").
- **Evidence:** `a11y1-home-390_200_html.png` (focused "Synthetic Documentary B" cut at the right edge),
  `a11y1-home-a-…`, `a11y1-home-watch-…`, `a11y1-library-390_200_html.png`, `a11y1.json`.
- **Root cause:** the Rail Focus Rule's region (`home.css:286`): `scroll-padding-inline-end:max(var(--pad),
  calc(100% - var(--pad) - max(var(--cw),var(--wide-max)) - 0.5rem))`. At 200 % `--cw` is 240 px and `--wide-max`
  360 px (`home.css:505`, rem-based), so the calc is negative (390 - 40 - 360 - 16) and the padding collapses to
  `--pad` (40 px): the "optimal region" is 40..350 px. The next card rests at 308 px, overlaps that region by 42 px,
  so Chromium treats it as already visible and does not scroll. With mixed widths (240 and 312 px) there is no
  single scroll-padding that both contains the widest card and excludes a peeking one at this size.
- **Minimum correction (tested):** a focus handler in `home.js` (progressive; JavaScript-off keeps today's
  behaviour):
  `document.addEventListener('focusin', e => { const a = e.target; if (!a.matches?.('.rail a.card')) return;
  const r = a.getBoundingClientRect(), b = a.closest('.rail').getBoundingClientRect();
  a.scrollIntoView({block:'nearest', inline: (r.left >= b.left + 4 && r.right <= b.right - 4) ? 'nearest' : 'start',
  behavior: 'instant'}); });`
  Injected into the live page this gave **0 failing stops** at 360, 390, 390@200 % and 430@200 % (`a11y1-jsfix.json`).
  The CSS-only alternative (region one `--cw` wide) was also tested and **rejected**: it fixes 200 % but regresses
  100 % (32 to 46 clipped stops at 360 and 390, square covers end-aligned at -6..174). This departs from DESIGN.md's
  "No script" note for the Rail Focus Rule; that note should change with the fix. Do not change the harness assertion.
- **Regression risk:** low to medium (scroll on focus in every rail; check snap and the Show more focus move, part
  12). **Existing issue:** RC1 A11Y-1 (MEDIA-2.0-RC1, draft #165); RC2-STATE section 5.

### U-04 Focused cards partly hidden under the tab bar or the sticky header

- **Class / severity:** CONFIRMED, P3. **Beta-blocking: no.** Never wholly hidden (WCAG 2.4.11 AA passes); it fails
  the house rule "nothing focused hides under the tab bar" and 2.4.12 (AAA).
- **Measured:** 320x640@200 %: focused cards' bottom 9 to 30 px under the tab bar (Home, all filters). Library at
  every width (360, 390, 390@200 %): Shift+Tab leaves the card's art top under the header/`.chips` ledger
  (`elementFromPoint` hits `div.mast` or `a.chip`). Home at 320@200 %: under `nav.places.filters`.
- **Root cause:** only Home sets `scroll-padding-top` (`home.css:1006`, `html:has(.mast.at-home)`); Library's sticky
  ledger (`home.css:1008-1015`) has none; vertical scrolling is skipped when a card is partly visible.
- **Correction:** `html:has(.lib-head){scroll-padding-top:<ledger height>}`; U-03's handler (`block:'nearest'`)
  then respects the page's scroll-padding top and bottom. **Evidence:** `a11y1.json` `obscured`. **Issue:** none.

### U-05 Hero facts and controls overlap at 320 px and at large text

- **Class / severity:** CONFIRMED, P2. **Beta-blocking: no** (320 px phones are rare; 390@200 % is clean).
- **Measured:** `.hero-facts` bottom overlaps `.hero-ctl` top by **40 px at 320@200 %** and **33 px at 195x422**; at
  320x568 (100 %) "2005 · Movies and TV" visibly collides with the progress line and "81 minutes left", and Resume
  sits behind the tab bar on the first screen.
- **Evidence:** `home-default-320.png`, `home-default-320-200pct.png`, `home-default-195-reflow.png`, `text200.json`.
- **Root cause:** `home.css:424` positions `.hero-ctl` absolutely at the band's foot; `.hero-foot` reserves a fixed
  `padding-bottom:3.375rem` (`home.css:411-412`) for one line of controls. When the time and button wrap to two
  lines, the controls grow upward into the facts.
- **Correction:** put the controls in flow when they cannot fit one row, for example `@media (max-width:359px)
  {.hero-ctl{position:static;padding:0}}` plus the same under large text (a container query on `.hero-card` in
  `em`), keeping the at-rest composition at 360+ unchanged.
- **Regression risk:** low (narrow widths only). **Issue:** none; DESIGN.md "below 360px no poster" is stale (U-22).

## (e) Clean screens and states

- No sideways page scroll at any of the nine sizes in `ok` on any page (except U-10, U-11 at large text).
- Search: empty, results (count first, one panel per library, row-wide links), no results ("Ask for it" rows saying
  "Opens the request website"), invalid query, one library unavailable (warning inside the page, count of the rest),
  none answering ("Not searched", never "No results"): all as specified.
- Title page (film, audiobook, comic): display title, Cream action, "Opens in" panel, About; unavailable library is a
  503 warning page with Try again.
- Title sheet: opens from a card by Enter and from the hero picture by click; focus goes to Close; Escape, the close
  button and browser Back all close it and **return focus to the opener** (`interact.json` `sheet`); Tab cycles
  inside (and to the browser chrome, native modal behaviour).
- Scroll-driven hero (motion allowed): sticks under the slim header at 58 px, shrinks 1:1 (452 -> 393 -> 273 -> 173 ->
  140 px at 0/140/260/360/460 px of scroll), the stack fades out, the strip fades in, then leaves; scrolling up 60 px
  brings the places and the full tab bar back; reaching the end and the top restores them. The next shelf rides
  directly under the window (no jump; CLS 0).
- Bottom nav: shrinks to 0.84 with labels hidden on the way down, restores on the way up, at the end and on focus;
  Reduce Motion changes state instantly with no transition.
- Skip link: first Tab, visible, Enter moves focus to `main#main`.
- Empty and edge identities: member-nolib (Comics only, no Movies and TV named), member-c "Not set up yet" with
  "What to do next", synthetic-admin "No library yet", signed-out portal page; Continue page in every mode.
- Error modes `down`, `500`, `malformed`, `slow`: one consistent member sentence, Try again, no raw error, host or
  software name; struck-through Library chips and Home filters said "unavailable" to screen readers.
- Offline page, Account, Help, Help topic and "If something doesn't open": consistent grouped rows and panels.
- Artwork keeps native shapes in rails, grids and search thumbnails (2:3, 13:20, square, 16:9); letter frames for
  missing art.

**Cohesion verdict.** Inside the shell the product reads as one: one type family, one ink/cream/stone palette, one
yellow for selection and focus, grouped rows on Search, Request, Me, Account and Help, one button family (`.kbtn`,
Cream primary). The seams a member can see are U-17 (404 and error pages in the Title Card), U-11 (TV page field and
"TV and Movies"), U-14 (two filter vocabularies) and U-18 (two warning layouts). A first-time member on Home knows
where they are (lockup, Home tab lit, the hero names the next thing) and what to do next (Resume), but not where to
ask for something or get help (U-15).

## (g) Not tested

- **Safari / iOS WebKit** (scroll timelines need Safari 26; older Safari gets the plain scroll), **VoiceOver**,
  TalkBack, real Dynamic Type, real phones, the installed PWA (standalone mode, safe areas, status bar), TVs.
- **Request ask pages and My requests** (`/request/watch|books|comics`, `/request/mine`): 404 on the dev stack
  (brokers off); live has them on. Needs a fixture or broker mock render.
- **Me -> My profile, Customize, Account & Security flows** (`HOME_MEMBER_STORE`, `HOME_SECURITY_FLOWS` off on dev
  and live).
- **Service worker, update banner, offline fallback in a real offline state**: `pwa_run.py`, `motion_run.py`,
  `skip_link_run.py` and `run_ui.py` were **not run** because they start their own servers on ephemeral ports,
  outside the ports this audit was allowed (8096, 8480, 18096 to 18099). The skip link and view-transition behaviour
  were checked by my own scripts on 8096 instead.
- Pressed (`:active`) states (headless cannot hold a touch); real Jellyfin logos and wide backdrops (fixtures have
  none, so the hero shows a cropped poster); proxy compression on the live host; true browser text-size settings
  (Chromium's preference was not honoured headless).
