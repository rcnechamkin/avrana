## iPhone and iPad
### Safari
store: none
url: none
1. Open https://avrana.net in Safari and sign in. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
4. Keep the name Avrana and tap Add. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
5. Open Avrana from your Home Screen. If it asks you to sign in, sign in again. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]

## Android
### Chrome
store: none
url: none
1. Open https://avrana.net in Chrome and sign in. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
2. Tap the three dots in the top right. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
3. Tap Add to Home screen. If you see Install app, choose that. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
4. Confirm the name and tap Add. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
5. Open Avrana from your Home screen. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]

## Computer
### Chrome or Edge
store: none
url: none
