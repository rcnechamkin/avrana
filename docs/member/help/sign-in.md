3. After you sign in, you land on the Avrana home page. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]

## If sign-in loops or fails
### Try these in order
store: none
url: none
1. Check the address bar. It should say avrana.net. If it shows another address, go to https://avrana.net. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
2. Close the tab and open https://avrana.net again. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
3. Open a private or incognito window and sign in there. If that works, clear the site data for avrana.net in your browser settings and try again. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
4. Turn off any content blocker for avrana.net and try again. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
5. Check the date and time on your device. A wrong clock can break sign-in. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
6. Forgot your password? [OWNER DECISION D-13: what to tell members about password recovery] [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
7. Still stuck? [OWNER DECISION D-1: where to send a help request] [UNVERIFIED on a real device]

## On a shared device
### Signing out
store: none
url: none
1. Open Account and choose Sign out. It signs you out of Avrana Media on this device and clears what Avrana Media saved here. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
2. Apps and websites you signed in to separately stay signed in. Sign out inside each one. [UNVERIFIED on a real device]
